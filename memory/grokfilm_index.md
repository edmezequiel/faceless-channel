# Grokfilm Index: Dicionário Cinematográfico para IA Generativa

Este documento serve como o vocabulário oficial estruturado para o `generative_cinematographer`. Ao construir prompts para Midjourney, Runway, Kling, ou Sora, o agente DEVE utilizar estes termos exatos para otimizar os *latent spaces* dos modelos.

## 1. Camera Work (Trabalho de Câmera)
**Enquadramentos:** Extreme Long Shot, Long Shot, Full Shot, Medium Shot, Medium Close-Up, Close-Up Shot, Extreme Close-Up, Choker Shot, Insert Shot, Two-Shot, Three-Shot, Cowboy Shot, Head Shot, Profile Shot, Trunk Shot.
**Ângulos:** Eye-Level Shot, High Angle Shot, Low Angle Shot, Bird's Eye View, Worm's Eye View, Dutch Angle (Canted Frame), Overhead Shot, Reverse Angle.
**Movimentos:** Dolly Shot (Dolly-In, Dolly-Out), Tracking Shot, Pan Shot, Whip Pan, Tilt Shot, Zoom Shot, Crash Zoom, Dolly Zoom (Vertigo Effect), Crane Shot, Jib Shot, Pedestal Shot.
**Estilos de Operação:** Steadicam Shot, Handheld Shot, Aerial Shot, Helicopter Shot, Snorricam, Locked-Down Shot.

## 2. Lighting (Iluminação)
**Estilos:** Chiaroscuro, Rembrandt Lighting, High-Key Lighting, Low-Key Lighting, Silhouette, Motivated Lighting, Practical Lighting, Available Light, Day for Night, Night for Night.
**Configurações:** Three-Point Lighting, Key Light, Fill Light, Backlight, Kicker Light, Hair Light, Rim Lighting, Cross Lighting, Split Lighting, Broad Lighting, Short Lighting, Loop Lighting, Butterfly Lighting, Uplighting, Side Lighting, Top Lighting, Frontal Lighting.
**Cores e Atmosferas:** Golden Hour, Blue Hour, Color Temperature (warm/cool), Dappled Light, Edge Light, Eye Light, Gobo Lighting, Neon Lighting, Mood Lighting, Underwater Lighting.
**Efeitos Óticos:** Lens Flare, Accent Light.

## 3. Composition (Composição)
**Regras e Foco:** Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio, Symmetry, Asymmetry, Leading Lines, Framing Within Frame, Negative Space, Headroom, Lead Room.
**Profundidade:** Shallow Focus, Deep Focus, Depth of Field, Figure-Ground Relationship, Visual Weight, Repetition and Pattern.
**Mise-en-Scène:** Organização completa de cenografia, figurino e bloqueio no quadro.

## 4. Editing Techniques (Para modelos de vídeo temporal)
**Transições e Cortes:** Cross-Cutting, Match Cut, Match on Action, Jump Cut, Smash Cut, Cutaway, Cut-In, Dissolve, Fade In/Out, Wipe, Iris.
**Tempo e Ritmo:** Time-Lapse, Slow Motion, Fast Motion, Reverse Motion, Freeze Frame, Long Take, One-er (Oner), Parallel Editing.
**Montagem:** Soviet Montage, Intellectual Montage, Metric Montage, Rhythmic Montage.
**Regras Espaciais:** 180-Degree Rule, 30-Degree Rule, Eyeline Match, Cutting on Look.

## 5. Visual Effects & Post-Processing (Efeitos e Textura)
**Texturas Clássicas:** Vintage Film Look, Sepia Tone, Film Grain, Desaturation, Color Grading, Color Correction, Black and White.
**Efeitos de Câmera/Lente:** Parallax Effect, Vignette, Bokeh, Split Diopter, Lens Distortion, Anamorphic Flares, Chromatic Aberration, Motion Blur.
**Técnicas Físicas/Mistas:** Practical Effects, Stop Motion, Miniatures, Forced Perspective, Matte Painting.

## 6. Genres & Styles (Estética de Gênero)
- **Horror:** Gothic Horror, J-Horror, Elevated Horror, Body Horror, Giallo.
- **Nostalgia/Retro:** Synthwave Retrowave, Steampunk, Cyberpunk, Afrofuturism.
- **Realismo:** Cinema Vérité, Italian Neorealism, Dogme 95, Mumblecore, Found Footage.
- **Estilo Dramático/Ação:** Film Noir, Neo-Noir, German Expressionism, French New Wave, Surrealism, Wuxia, Heroic Bloodshed.

---
**Instrução Crítica para o Agente:**
Nunca invente termos genéricos (ex: "iluminação bonita", "câmera de longe"). Substitua-os sempre pelos termos técnicos deste dicionário (ex: "Rembrandt lighting", "Extreme Long Shot") para máxima aderência no prompt generativo.
